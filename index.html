01 — Market Research
Consumer Behaviour · Omnichannel Strategy · Statistical Research
Should Amazon Enter Physical Retail in India?
A full-cycle mixed-methods research study answering whether a digital-first platform should open physical stores in urban India. Used 30 in-depth interviews to surface 7 latent consumer variables, then validated through chi-square tests, PCA factor analysis, logistic regression (Nagelkerke R² = 0.73), and K-means cluster segmentation. Identified the "Trust-Led Omnichannel Buyer" as the primary ICP — representing 35–40% of the urban market — and recommended a selective phased rollout strategy anchored in reassurance, not speed.

02 — Pricing & Revenue Strategy
Online Travel · Pricing Analytics · Conversion Strategy
Hotel Booking Window Strategy: Pricing, Timing & Conversion
Asked how a global online travel platform could grow revenue per booking by shaping when users book, not just how many book. Analysed 49,061 bookings across 5 cities and 9 star ratings: average room rates fall 30% as check-in nears ($170 → $119), yet 48% of bookings land in the final 7 days. Confirmed it was real discounting, not customer mix, via regression controls (p < 0.001), segmented pricing behaviour across 50 city × star segments, sized a $33K–$66K booking-value opportunity, and designed a segmented urgency-messaging rules engine.

03 — Commercial Strategy
PropTech · Marketplace · Performance Review
Real Estate Marketplace: Commercial Performance Review
Independent review of a MENA property marketplace from inception (2019–2022), across demand, client and revenue data. Visits grew 292% to 13.8M a quarter, but underneath the growth: a third of retained clients saw revenue decline, revenue per listing fell 32% as listings outpaced clients 4×, and paid app lead conversion dropped from 20.6% to 7.4% despite cheaper clicks. Recommended package restructuring with premium add-ons, a pre-renewal ROI playbook for at-risk mature accounts, and shifting paid spend to cost per qualified lead.
